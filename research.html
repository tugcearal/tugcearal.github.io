Research

Cultural diversity & adolescent development

Her work sits at the intersection of developmental and intergroup psychology, examining how cultural diversity is experienced and negotiated in schools and families.

Focus areas

  • Out-group prosocial behavior

    How solidarity toward out-groups develops and is expressed during adolescence.

  • Cultural diversity & socialization

    Classroom climate and family ethnic-racial socialization as everyday learning about difference.

  • Ethnic identity development

    Exploration, engagement, and inclusivity in how young people form ethnic identity.

  • Intergroup attitudes

    Adolescents' willingness to support refugee and immigrant-origin peers, and what shapes it.
