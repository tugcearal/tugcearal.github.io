Research

Cultural diversity & adolescent development

Her work sits at the intersection of developmental and intergroup psychology, examining how cultural diversity is experienced and negotiated in schools and families. She is a Postdoctoral Researcher and Lecturer at the University of Potsdam and a Postdoctoral Fellow at the College for Interdisciplinary Educational Research (CIDER).

Focus areas

  • Out-group prosocial behavior — how solidarity toward out-groups develops and is expressed during adolescence.
  • Cultural diversity & socialization — classroom climate and family ethnic-racial socialization as everyday learning about difference.
  • Ethnic identity development — exploration, engagement, and inclusivity in how young people form ethnic identity.
  • Intergroup attitudes — adolescents' willingness to support refugee and immigrant-origin peers, and what shapes it.
